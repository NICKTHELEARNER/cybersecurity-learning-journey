# Write-ups

This directory contains write-ups of interesting challenges, labs, and problems I have solved during my cybersecurity studies.

The purpose of these write-ups is to document my reasoning and learning process, not just the final solution.

## What I Document

A write-up may include:

* The objective
* Initial observations
* Reconnaissance
* My hypotheses
* What I tried
* Mistakes and failed approaches
* How I solved the problem
* Why the solution worked
* What I learned
* Related concepts

## Sources

Write-ups are based on authorized training environments such as:

* pwn.college
* PortSwigger Web Security Academy
* CTFs
* Other intentionally vulnerable environments

I will not publish sensitive information from private programs or disclose vulnerabilities in real systems without authorization.
